# PagePeace Preliminary Name Review

Date: 2026-08-15

An exact-name web search did not surface an obvious browser extension, ad blocker, or software product using `PagePeace`. Some unrelated lowercase uses appear in event/promoter indexes. This is a useful preliminary screen, not legal clearance.

Before public launch, the owner should commission or conduct a formal confusing-similarity search covering at least:

- `PagePeace`, `Page Peace`, `PeacePage`, and phonetic variants.
- Software, downloadable browser extensions, privacy services, cybersecurity, and related classes.
- The shield-plus design mark and visually similar security/privacy marks.
- Canada, the United States, and every intended sales/distribution territory.
- Common-law uses, app stores, corporate registries, domains, and social handles—not only registered marks.

Official starting points:

- Canadian Trademarks Database: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch?lang=eng
- USPTO Trademark Search: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/

No statement in this review is legal advice or a guarantee that the name is registrable or non-infringing.
